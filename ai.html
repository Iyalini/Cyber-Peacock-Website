Artificial Intelligence & Cybersecurity

AI is transforming every aspect of our lives, but with great power comes great risk. Discover how hackers exploit AI systems and how cybersecurity protects them.

▶Start Learning
Module Content

What You Will Learn in This Module

This pathway breaks down AI cybersecurity into digestible, engaging lessons. No prior tech experience needed.

01

How AI Systems Actually Work

Learn the basics of machine learning and neural networks in plain English. Understand how AI "thinks" so you can spot when it's being manipulated.

02

Recognising Deepfakes

Develop your eye for spotting AI-generated images, videos, and audio. Practise with interactive examples and learn the "SIFT" verification method.

03

AI & Your Personal Data

Discover what data AI apps collect about you, where it goes, and how to review permissions on your devices to minimise your digital footprint.

04

Ethical AI Use

Explore what it means to use AI responsibly, covering bias, misinformation, academic integrity, and the ethical rules real AI developers follow.

05

Defending Against AI Attacks

Learn practical cybersecurity defences: strong authentication, phishing detection, safe AI tool usage, and how to report suspicious activity.

06

AI in Cybersecurity Defence

See how the good guys fight back, AI is also used to detect malware, flag fraud in milliseconds, and protect critical infrastructure around the world.
