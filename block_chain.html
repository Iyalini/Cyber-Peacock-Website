Blockchain & Cybersecurity

Blockchain promises a more secure internet — but it's not invincible. Learn how this revolutionary technology works, where it's vulnerable, and how cybersecurity keeps it trustworthy.

▶Start Learning
Module Content

What You Will Learn in This Module

From the basics of cryptography to real-world scam defence, this module equips you with the knowledge to navigate the blockchain world safely.

01

Cryptography Fundamentals

Understand hashing, public/private key pairs, and digital signatures — the building blocks of all blockchain security.

02

How Blockchain Achieves Trust

Explore consensus mechanisms (Proof of Work vs Proof of Stake) and why decentralisation is such a powerful security feature.

03

Spotting Crypto Scams

Learn the red flags of rug pulls, fake exchanges, NFT fraud, and phishing links — with interactive scam-spotting quizzes.

04

Safe Wallet Practices

Discover hardware wallets, cold storage, seed phrase security, and how to safely interact with decentralised applications.

05

Blockchain Beyond Crypto

Discover how blockchain is used in healthcare records, supply chains, voting systems, and digital identity — and the cybersecurity implications of each.

06

Smart Contract Security

Understand what makes smart contracts vulnerable and why security audits are critical — an emerging career skill in high demand globally.
