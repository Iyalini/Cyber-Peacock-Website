Internet of Things (IoT) & Cybersecurity

The Internet of things connects our physical world to the digital one. but every "smart" device is a potential open door. Learn how Iot echosystems function, where the security gaps lie, and how to protect the hyper connected future.

▶Start Learning
Module Content

What You Will Learn in This Module

From securing home thermostats to protecting industrial power grids, this module equips you with the strategies needed to defend the vast network of interconnected devices.

01

IoT Architecture & Communication

Understand the "Sensing, Network, and Application" layers. Learn how devices talk via protocols like MQTT, Zigbee, and Bluetooth Low Energy(BLE) and the risks inherent in each.

02

The Vulnerability of "Smart" Things

Explore why IoT is a prime target: limited processing power for encryption, hardcoded passwords, and the lack of standardized update mechanisms.

03

Defending the Edge & the Cloud

Learn about Edge Computing security vs. Cloud security. Discover how gateways act as firewalls for "dumb" devices and how to secure the data pipelines between them.

04

IoT Botnets & Large-Scale Attacks

Analyze famous breaches like the Mirai Botnet. Learn how unsecured cameras and routers can be weaponized into DDoS attacks that take down entire sections of the internet.

05

Industrial IoT (IIoT) & Critical Infrastructure

Discover the high stakes of "Smart Cities," healthcare devices (IoMT), and manufacturing. Understand the cybersecurity implications when a hack moves from stealing data to affecting physical safety.

06

Securing the Ecosystem: Best Practices

Master the fundamentals of IoT hygiene: changing default credentials, network segmentation (keeping your fridge off your main Wi-Fi), and the role of Zero Trust architecture in connected environments.
